Does It Pencil?

PencilBot is a neutral validator. It checks what a deal states against the evidence, and records each item as verified, not verified or unknown.

  • Stated
  • Verified
  • Not verified
  • Unknown

What Pencil is

The pencil draws the line.

Black and white

A fact is verified or it is not. Anything in between is recorded as unknown, never rounded up.

No side

Pencil does not prospect, sell, negotiate or set terms. It has no stake in the answer.

The ledger is the record

Every finding is written down with its evidence, so the work can be defended later.

What Pencil does not do

The people involved decide.

  • It does not prospect, sell or negotiate.
  • It does not set a value, a price or terms.
  • It does not represent the buyer, the seller or a broker.
  • It does not make the decision. It shows what holds.

Status

Phase 1: testnet.

PencilBot is being tested and its method written down before it is offered as a service. It started in commercial real estate, where a deal “pencils” when the numbers hold up on paper.
